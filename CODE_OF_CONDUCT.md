# Code of Conduct

affectd follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email Rayan Alyasi at <rayanalyasi@gmail.com>. We'll reply within a week and keep your identity private.

If your report is about Rayan, contact Omar ([@omarg-dev](https://github.com/omarg-dev)) instead, using the contact details on his GitHub profile.

Maintainers decide on consequences using the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), from a private warning to a permanent ban. A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in deciding it.

If affectd joins the OpenSSF, its code of conduct process will also apply.
